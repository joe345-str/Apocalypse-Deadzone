HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective

  • Survive endless zombie waves
  • Kill zombies to earn points
  • Each wave gets bigger and faster
  • Don't let health reach zero
  • Beat the high score

⌨️ Keyboard & Mouse

WASD or ↑↓←→  Move

Mouse  Aim direction

Click / Space  Shoot

R  Reload    V  Switch view

P / Esc  Pause

📱 Mobile Touch

  • D-Pad bottom-left — move
  • 🔫 SHOOT button — fire
  • ↺ RELOAD button — reload
  • 3P / 1P — switch view mode
  • ⏸ in HUD — pause

👁️ View Modes

  • 3RD PERSON — top-down. Camera follows you. Mouse aims.
  • 1ST PERSON — FPS raycaster. Red crosshair. Shoot what you see.
  • Switch anytime with V

❤️ HUD Indicators

  • Red bar — player health
  • Green pips — bullets in clip
  • Dim pips — spent ammo (reload!)
  • Orange number — current wave
  • Top right — score

🧟 Zombie Types

  • 🟢 Walker — slow, 2 hits
  • 🔴 Runner — fast, 1 hit kill
  • ⚫ Tank — slow, 6 hits
  • 🟣 Boss — wave 5+, 20 hits
  • All speed up each wave
GAME MANUAL

Overview

DEAD ZONE is a dual-view zombie survival shooter. Switch freely between a top-down 3rd person view and a full DDA raycaster first-person perspective. Fight endless waves using your pistol — manage ammo and survive.

Scoring

Walker Kill+100
Runner Kill+150
Tank Kill+200
Boss Kill+1,000
Wave Clear Bonus+500 × wave

Zombie Stats

🟢 WalkerHP:2 · Spd:1.2 · Dmg:1
🔴 RunnerHP:1 · Spd:2.8 · Dmg:1
⚫ TankHP:6 · Spd:0.8 · Dmg:2
🟣 Boss (wave 5+)HP:20 · Spd:1.5 · Dmg:3

Wave Progression

Wave 1 starts with 8 zombies. Each wave adds 3 more. Speed increases 5% per wave. Bosses appear from wave 5. Health restores +30 HP between waves.
